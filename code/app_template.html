How AVECAGRO works

Step 1 – P1 Intake & Validation: turns free text (English/Hindi/Hinglish) into a structured farm profile, rejects invalid or off-topic input, flags impossible or contradictory sensor values and lists missing information.

Retrieval (RAG): a keyword/tag retriever scores 27 curated agronomy passages by state, season, crop and topic and passes the top passages to the next prompt.

Step 2 – P2 Crop Recommendation: recommends up to 3 crops grounded only in the retrieved passages, with citations, soil status (measured / estimated / unknown) and confidence.

Step 3 – P3 Farm Plan & Calendar: week-by-week tasks, irrigation and nutrient notes that react to rain forecasts and soil-moisture readings.

Step 4 – P4 Pest Alert & Advisor: alert level, likely pests with evidence, IPM steps, drone-spray suitability and when to call an expert; never gives pesticide doses.

Routing: planning a new crop → P2 → P3 → P4 watch-list · crop standing with a problem → P4 only · crop standing, no problem → P3 + P4 watch-list · invalid or outside India → stop after P1.

Model: Claude, called through the Claude artifact runtime (sample API, model tier "default"). Knowledge base passages are summarised from public agronomy guidance (ICAR / state agricultural university packages of practice, Govt. of India schemes) and must be validated with local experts. AVECAGRO gives guidance, not guarantees — always confirm with your KVK or agriculture officer.
