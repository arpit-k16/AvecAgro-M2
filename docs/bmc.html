AvecAgro — Business Model Canvas

Team PE-B49-23 · AvecAgro · Project: AvecAgro – AI-Powered Precision Agriculture · SCSE4041 Mini Project, Milestone 2 · Supporting document

Key partners

  • Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) and state agricultural universities: validate the knowledge base and advice
  • Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) and co-operatives: reach and onboarding
  • IoT sensor makers and drone/rover service providers (custom hiring centres, Namo Drone Didi operators)
  • Soil-testing labs (Soil Health Card network)
  • LLM / cloud provider (currently Claude; open-source model planned for offline use)
  • Weather data providers (IMD forecasts)

Key activities

  • Prompt engineering and testing of the 4-step pipeline (intake, crop choice, plan, pest alert)
  • Curating and expert-checking the agricultural knowledge base (RAG)
  • Integrating sensor, weather and drone data
  • Field pilots with farmers; collecting feedback

Key resources

  • Curated knowledge base (27 passages now; to grow by state/crop)
  • Prompt library P1–P4 with test suite (14 cases)
  • Web app and LLM access
  • Agronomy advisors and the student team

Value proposition

  • Personal advice in minutes: crop choice, week-by-week plan and pest alert from the farmer's own location, season, budget, water and soil/sensor data
  • Trustworthy: every crop and pest claim cites a knowledge-base source; measured vs estimated soil is always shown; no invented readings, no yield guarantees, no pesticide doses
  • Saves money and water: low-input crops for low budgets, irrigation postponed before rain, Soil Health Card based fertiliser
  • Farmer's language: English, Hindi and Hinglish
  • Safe on bad data: flags impossible or contradictory sensor values and asks questions instead of guessing

Customer relationships

  • Self-service web/mobile assistant
  • Assisted onboarding through FPOs and KVK field staff
  • Seasonal reminders and pest alerts
  • Escalation to KVK / Kisan Call Centre for expert help

Channels

  • Web app (now); Android app and WhatsApp bot (planned)
  • FPO meetings, KVK demos, krishi melas
  • Agri-input dealers and drone service partners

Customer segments

  • Primary: small and marginal farmers (86% of Indian holdings, Agriculture Census 2015–16)
  • FPOs managing many member farms
  • Agri-extension workers and KVK staff
  • Drone/rover service providers needing spray decisions
  • Agri-input companies and NGOs running farmer programmes

Cost structure

  • LLM API / hosting cost per advice request (4 model calls per full run)
  • Knowledge-base curation and expert review
  • App development and maintenance
  • Field pilots, farmer training and outreach
  • IoT/drone integration and partner onboarding

Revenue streams

  • Free basic advice for individual farmers (adoption first)
  • Subscription for FPOs (per-member fee for alerts, dashboards and bulk plans)
  • Commission on booked services: soil tests, drone spraying, rover inspection
  • B2B licences / white-label for agri-input firms, NGOs and state programmes
  • Paid premium plans: sensor integration and multi-plot planning
Prototype status (Milestone 2): 4 prompts + retrieval working in a web app; IoT, drone and rover data are entered as text for now. Figures and partner roles are plans to be validated in Milestone 3.
